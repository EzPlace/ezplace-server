Daily streak
Spend 15 active minutes on the site each day to claim your daily bonus: 2 PlaceBux times your streak length, up to 50 a day. Miss a day and the streak resets. The shop sells a 48-hour window and Streak Passes that protect it.
Fishing
Trade: swap fish and PlaceBux with another player from the Trade button on the Fishing screen. Maps: Sunny Pier is free; Maps unlock once for PlaceBux: Coral Reef 150, Midnight Abyss 500, Frostbite Lake 1000, Misty Bayou 2000, Magma Lagoon 4000. Every map has its own fish and casting costs 5 PlaceBux everywhere. Each map has its own scenery and catches, and each of the two new maps has 9 fish you cannot catch anywhere else. Rods wear out: the free thin wooden rod snaps after 55 casts, then you buy a new one (wooden 10, plastic 20, hollow steel 50, carbon fiber 100 PlaceBux, lasting 55 / 100 / 500 / 1000 casts). Casting costs 5 PlaceBux. Sell what you reel in from your bucket: common catches are worth 1 or 2, legendary ones hundreds, and the rarest items up to 2,500.
